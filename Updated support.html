Common Questions

How do deliveries work?

Sellers list items for pickup. A nearby driver accepts the job, picks the item up, and drops it off in person — nothing is shipped. You can track pickup/drop-off status from My Orders.

Something went wrong with my order — what do I do?

Open My Orders and use "Report a problem," or file a claim from Settings → Claims. Our team reviews the pickup/drop-off photo and video evidence to resolve it.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Delete Account. Your account is deactivated immediately; logging back in within 30 days restores it, after which it's permanently deleted.

How do refunds work?

If a claim is confirmed, a refund is issued as part of resolving it. There's no separate refund request — it's handled through the claims process above.

I want to report a user, post, or comment.

Tap the "•••" menu on the post/comment, or the Report button on a profile, and describe the issue. Our team reviews every report.
