Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

What We Collect

Account info: your name, email, username, and password (handled securely by our authentication provider — we never see your raw password).

Photos: listing photos, your profile avatar and banner, and pickup/drop-off evidence photos and short videos captured during deliveries.

Location: precise location when you set a pickup or drop-off address, and while a driver is actively completing a delivery, so we can confirm they're physically at the right place. We only collect location "while using" the app — never in the background.

Messages: text, images, files, and voice messages you send through in-app chat with other users or with support.

Payment info: when you pay for a delivery, a subscription, or a shop item, your payment details are collected and processed directly by Stripe or Apple — we never see or store your full card number.

AI chat messages: questions you send to HaulSpot Support or Vayl are processed by our AI provider to generate a response, and stored so your conversation history can load.

How We Use It

To operate the marketplace and delivery features, verify drivers are at the correct location, process payments, respond to support requests, enforce our Trust & Safety rules (including reviewing reports and claims), and improve the app.

Who We Share It With

Supabase (our backend/database/storage provider), Stripe and Apple (payment processing), and our AI provider (for Support and Vayl responses). We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Your Choices

You can make your account private, hide your follower/following counts, block other users, and delete your account at any time from Settings. Deleting your account deactivates it immediately; you can restore it by logging back in within a grace period, after which your data is permanently removed.

Children

HaulSpot is not directed to children, and you must be at least 18 years old to create an account, since the app facilitates real payments and in-person delivery.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the in-app Support chat, or to the contact email listed on our App Store page.
