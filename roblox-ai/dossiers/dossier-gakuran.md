## Core loop (timed estimates ❓)
There are **no quests or required progression** ✅. It's a sandbox where players make their own goals.
1. **Roll your character** on first join: style, height, face, ethnicity, clothes (1–2 min)
2. **Roam** the school and town and roleplay in safe zones (open-ended)
3. **Pick fights** in PvP areas: duels, gang brawls, defending turf (30–90 s per fight)
4. **Earn yen** from phone job shifts (passive, often in safe zones)
5. **Reroll** for a better style, height, or look (Robux or code rerolls), then go back to step 3

The real "progression" is **player skill** (combos, parries, weaving), **social status** (gangs and reputation), and **gacha luck** (rolling a top style and height).

| Input | Action | Confidence |
|---|---|---|

- **Green zones:** fighting is **disabled automatically** in safe areas (classrooms, music room, job sites). ✅
- **Design identity:** fast, combo-driven melee that rewards reads: block → parry → punish, weaving, and guard breaks. Skill matters more than grinding. ✅
- Death/knockout rules: **not found.** Needs screenshots or footage.

## Why it works
1. **A strong fantasy:** "be the toughest delinquent at school" is a hugely popular anime/manga fantasy that very few Roblox games did well before it.
2. **Roleplay + PvP in one world:** green zones let social players hang out while fighters brawl nearby. Two audiences, one server, and the roleplayers give the fighters an audience.
3. **Skill-based combat:** parries, weaving, and guard breaks give a high skill ceiling, which makes for clips, rivalries, and long-term mastery without needing tons of content.
4. **Cheap gacha identity rolls:** style, height, and face rolls create attachment ("my 1% Boxing, 6'4" character") and constant tiny purchases. Rare rolls double as status.
5. **High-fidelity setting + 16+ rating:** it stands out visually and fits Roblox's 2026 push for older players. US 18+ spending also earns a higher DevEx rate.
6. **Player-made goals:** with no quests, the community makes the content (gangs, turf, drama), which keeps it cheap to run.

## Weak spots you could beat
- **No structured progression or quests:** solo and new players can bounce fast (bad for the 2026 retention-based algorithm).
- **Gang systems live on Discord, not in the game:** there's room for in-game crews, turf maps, and territory control.
- **Gacha balance frustration:** a top style is a 10% (Boxing) to 0.05% roll, and players with bad rolls can feel locked out.
- **The player count dropped from about 142K to about 8K:** content cadence and long-term goals are the likely problem.
